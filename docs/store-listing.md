# Store listing rules

For the App Store and Google Play listings. Ringgit Radar shows Nintendo's catalogue, prices and pictures without a licence, so the listing must make it impossible to mistake the app for Nintendo's, and must say where its data comes from. Comparable trackers are on both stores on these terms. Apple guidelines 4.1, 5.2.1 and 5.2.2 and Google Play's intellectual-property policy are the ones in play.

## Name and text

- **Name:** "Ringgit Radar" in every language. Never translated, never with a Nintendo word added.
- **Subtitle (App Store) and short description (Play):** no "Nintendo", "Switch", "eShop" or "Joy-Con". Draft: "Game prices in ringgit".
- **Keywords (App Store):** no Nintendo trademarks or game names. Use words such as ringgit, game prices, sale, deals, Malaysia, price history, wish list.
- **Long description:** may name "Nintendo Switch" once, only to say what the prices are for, and always in the same paragraph as: "Ringgit Radar is independent and unofficial. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo."
- No "official", "authorised" or "partner", and nothing that suggests the app sells games. There is no Buy button; games are bought on the Switch.

## Screenshots

- Each screenshot carries our own caption, in our type and colours, saying what the screen does ("Today's price and the lowest we've seen").
- No screenshot is filled by game art; the app's own layout, prices and history are the subject.
- No Nintendo logos, Switch hardware renders or Nintendo fonts, in screenshots or in the icon.

## If App Review asks for authorisation (guideline 5.2.2)

Reply along these lines, adjusting only the facts if they have changed:

> Ringgit Radar is an independent, unofficial price tracker for the Malaysian Nintendo eShop. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, and says so on first launch, beside its name in the header and sidebar, on its About page and in this listing.
>
> It shows the public catalogue information the eShop publishes (titles, publishers, release dates, prices and sale times) and keeps its own daily price history. Descriptions are shown as a short quote, labelled as the publisher's text. Pictures are not copied or stored: the app loads them from Nintendo's own image server. The app sells nothing and links to no purchase; games are bought on the Switch.
>
> Rights holders can ask for any content to be removed or corrected at ringgitradar@snitco.com. A removal is applied on our server with a removed-products list, which hides the product everywhere in the app at once, on every platform, without an app update. This contact and process are described in the app under About › Where our data comes from.
