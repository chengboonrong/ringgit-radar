# Back-translation record, 2026-11-22

The content clean-up added or changed 18 keys: the first-launch notice, the Unofficial chip and its screen-reader label, the publisher line on descriptions, the "Where our data comes from" page, and the privacy page's contact sentences. Each round, a fresh agent per language translated them back into English seeing only the translation and where it shows, never the English. Round 2 sent only the keys changed after round 1, plus `aboutUnofficial` in Malay, changed to keep the unofficial sentence the same everywhere.

Columns: key, what the agent read back, the problem, and the fix (the new text, or why the text stands). Keys not listed back-translated to the English meaning.

| Key | Back-translation | Problem | Fix |
