# Glossary: Ringgit Radar in Simplified Chinese and Malay

One term per concept, used everywhere the concept appears. Chinese is Simplified as Malaysian Chinese
readers write it; Malay is Bahasa Melayu as used in Malaysia (DBP spelling), never Indonesian.
"Ringgit Radar" is never translated. Money is always `RM 64.80` (never 令吉 or "ringgit" in a price), and
times are Malaysia time in every language.

| Concept (English) | zh | ms | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sale (a time-limited price cut; the Sale tab) | 特价 | Promosi | zh: 特价 is the plain word on Malaysian Chinese shop signs and Shopee/Lazada; 促销 reads as a marketing campaign, 折扣 is the discount itself. ms: Malaysian shops and apps say "promosi" for a time-limited cut; "Jualan" also means sales volume, which could read as "best-selling", against the honesty rules. |
| On sale | 特价中 | Dalam promosi | Follows "sale". |
| Sale ends {date} | 特价截止：{date} | Promosi sehingga {date} / Hingga {date} | zh 截止 is the usual word for an end time on Malaysian Chinese notices. ms "sehingga" (until) is unambiguous about the future; "tamat" was rejected after back-translation because Malay has no tense and it can read as "ended". |
| % OFF chip | 省{percent}% | DISKAUN {percent}% | zh: "省10%" (save 10%) is the plain retail phrasing and matches 省 for ringgit saved. Rejected after back-translation: 减10% (减 usually means a fixed amount off) and 10%折扣 (read under the 折 convention, where 九折 = 10% off, it can look like "pay 10%"). ms: "diskaun" is the Malaysian spelling (Indonesian "diskon"); capitals like the English chip. |
| Regular price (the struck price) | 原价 | harga asal | Standard on Malaysian shopping sites in both languages. |
| Observed low / lowest price we've seen | 我们见过的最低价 | harga terendah yang pernah kami lihat | Keeps "we've seen": only our own record, never an all-time or store-wide low. Rejected zh 历史最低价 and ms "harga terendah sepanjang masa", which claim more than we know. The date is when that price was first seen: zh says {date}首次出现, never {date}起为此价 (that claims the price held since then). |
| Price history / our history | 价格记录 / 我们的记录 | sejarah harga / rekod kami | zh 记录 (record) rather than 历史 (history) because it is our own log; ms "rekod" is the Malaysian spelling (Indonesian "rekaman"). |
| Tracking began | 开始记录于 | Rekod bermula | Says when our own record starts, nothing more. |
| Wish List | 愿望单 | Senarai Hajat | zh: 愿望单 is the Simplified term Malaysian Chinese gamers see on Steam; 心愿单 is used by shopping apps and 愿望清单 is longer for a tab. ms: "Senarai Hajat" is the Malaysian term (Lazada MY); Indonesian "Daftar Keinginan" rejected. Never shorten it to "Hajat" alone (it also calls up "buang hajat"); the game-page button says "+ Senarai Hajat" and, once saved, "Disimpan". |
| Ranking | 排行 | Kedudukan | Always stated as by ringgit saved. zh 排行 alone, never 排行榜 with 热门/畅销. ms "Kedudukan" (Indonesian "Peringkat" rejected). |
| Ringgit saved | 省下的令吉 | ringgit yang dijimatkan | zh 令吉 only as a word in a sentence, never in a price. ms "jimat" is the everyday Malaysian word for saving money. |
| Savings (biggest savings) | 节省金额最高 | penjimatan terbesar | Follows "ringgit saved". |
| Highlights | 精选 | Sorotan | zh 精选 (picked items) is short for a tab; ms "Sorotan" is the Malaysian word for highlights (e.g. Astro, news sites). |
| Release date | 发售日期 | Tarikh keluaran | zh 发售 is the gaming word for a release; ms "tarikh keluaran" as on Malaysian product pages. |
| Recent Releases | 新近发售 | Keluaran Terkini | Follows "release". |
| Coming Soon | 即将发售 | Akan Datang | zh 即将发售 keeps the release sense (即将推出 is vaguer); ms "Akan Datang" is what Malaysian cinemas and stores print. |
| Pre-order | 预购 | Pra-tempah | zh standard; ms "tempah" is Malaysian for order/book (Indonesian "pesan" rejected). |
| NEW chip | 新品 | BAHARU | ms DBP spelling of "new" for products; "BARU" is also common but "BAHARU" is the Malaysian formal form. |
| Add-on | 追加内容 | Kandungan tambahan | zh 追加内容 is Nintendo's own Chinese term for DLC; ms "kandungan tambahan" (bare "tambahan" reads as "extra"). Downloadable Content: 可下载内容 / Kandungan Boleh Muat Turun. |
| Bundle | 套装 | Pakej | zh 套装 for a set sold together; ms "pakej" as Malaysian retail says. |
| Game | 游戏 | Permainan | Standard. |
| Unofficial / not Nintendo | 非官方 / 与任天堂无关，也未获其认可 | tidak rasmi / tiada hubungan dengan Nintendo dan tidak diiktiraf oleh Nintendo | Never 官方 or "rasmi" without the negation. Nintendo stays Latin in ms; zh uses 任天堂, the company's own Chinese name. |
| Retry | 重试 | Cuba lagi | Standard button words in Malaysian apps. |
| Search | 搜索 | Cari | Standard. |
| Filter | 筛选 | Tapis | zh standard; ms "tapis" as Malaysian apps (Shopee MY) use. |
| Sort | 排序 | Isih | ms "isih" is the Malaysian (DBP) word; Indonesian "urutkan" rejected. |
| Genre | 游戏类型 | Genre | zh 游戏类型, not bare 类型, which reads as the product type beside it; ms keeps "genre" (DBP lists it). |
| Platform | 平台 | Platform | ms word is the same. |
| Malaysia time | 马来西亚时间 | waktu Malaysia | Standard. |
| Store / eShop | 商店 / eShop | kedai / eShop | "Nintendo eShop" and "Switch" stay as product names. |
| Clear (reset filters) | 清除 | Set semula | ms "set semula" (reset) says what the button does; "kosongkan" (empty) was rejected for filters after back-translation. Clear search (a text field): 清除搜索 / Kosongkan carian, the usual wording for emptying a field; "Padam carian" read back as "delete search". |
